4 · LeversWhat each trick is worth to the house

Each lever is switched on by itself against a no-lever baseline, using the same players and the same random seed. The behavior effects are illustrative assumptions, listed below the table. The game odds never change; only player behavior does.

Assumptions behind each lever
  • Deposit-match bonus: +100% on the first buy (capped at one starter pack) and +20% on reloads (capped at half a pack). The bonus must be wagered 15× before it counts, and any unplayed bonus is forfeited when the player quits. The ad brings in 30% more sign-ups for the same marketing. Churn ×0.85 and reload chance +10 points, because "free" credits feel like value.
  • VIP levels: Silver/Gold/Platinum at 50k/250k/1M credits wagered, rebating 3%/6%/10% of session losses. VIP members churn ×0.7 and play 20% more days.
  • Referral credits: about 30% of players invite one friend. Both get 500 free credits. Half of the invited friends go on to buy.
  • Near-miss and jackpot visuals: sessions last 20% longer, churn ×0.85, reload chance +10 points. These cost nothing in payouts.
  • RTP 98% / 94% / 88%: same players, different payout tuning, no levers on.

6 · The lawWhy this is illegal off-market

Gambling = all three of these

Prize+Chance+Consideration= gambling
  • Consideration: players pay real money for credits through agents and payment apps.
  • Chance: the RNG decides each catch. Skill can't beat the math above.
  • Prize: off-market agents "redeem" credits for real money. That cash-out is what turns a game into unlicensed gambling.
  • California AB 831 (effective Jan 1, 2026) bans online games that simulate gambling and pay out cash prizes. It also reaches the payment processors and affiliates that knowingly support them.
  • Michigan's Gaming Control Board ordered the "Orion Stars" fish-game platform to stop operating in March 2025, and running unlicensed online gambling there is a felony.
  • No regulator audits these apps, so the RTP figures they claim (93–98%) are unverified.

The legal version (like Reef Rumble)

  • Remove the prize: credits have no cash value and can never be cashed out, transferred, or redeemed.
  • Sell credits only through app-store billing, never through agents or cash apps.
  • Publish the odds, show a clear "no real money" notice, and add spend limits.
  • Even credits-only casino apps face legal risk in some states (Washington's 2026 lawsuit against big social-casino apps), so get legal advice before selling anything.

Educational summary, not legal advice.

The house always wins: not on every bet, but on every million bets.
