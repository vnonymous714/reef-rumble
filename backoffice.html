    What this is

    This dashboard dramatizes the platform → distributor → agent → player chain described in public reporting: players load credits through an agent over a P2P app or crypto, play a ~94% RTP fish game, and try to cash out winnings. The money math matches the How the House Wins model, so the house always grinds ahead while most players lose.

    Why it's illegal: real money (consideration) + an RNG outcome (chance) + cash redemption (prize) = unlicensed gambling. California's AB 831 (Jan 1 2026) and Michigan's 2025 cease-and-desist against the "Orion Stars" platform are examples of enforcement. The top real-world complaint, shown here as stalled and denied payouts, is operators simply not paying winners.
